A real answer is a link, plus a period measured in months or years, plus what happens
at the end of it. It should cover the different kinds of data separately: account
records, child observations, photographs and video, voice recordings, and anything
derived from them.

A dodge is "as long as necessary for the purposes described," which is the phrase that
means no policy. "Indefinitely, or until you ask" is at least honest, and is a real
answer you can record and compare.

Copy the link into your sheet rather than the summary. Policies get revised, and a
dated link is what lets you tell whether the terms changed under you.
