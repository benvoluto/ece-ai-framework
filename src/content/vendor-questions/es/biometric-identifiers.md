A real answer names the feature and the technique: photo auto-tagging computes a
facial template, or check-in matches a face to a stored template, or nothing in the
product computes a template of any kind. It should also say where the template is
stored, how long it is kept and whether you can switch the feature off entirely.

A dodge talks about photographs rather than templates. "We don't do facial
recognition" is not the same as "we do not compute face geometry" — grouping a
child's photos by who is in them requires a template whatever the marketing calls it.
"Voice search" and "smart tagging" are the phrases to push on.

If the answer is yes, this needs separate, specific, revocable parental consent
before a single child is enrolled in the feature, not a line in the handbook.
