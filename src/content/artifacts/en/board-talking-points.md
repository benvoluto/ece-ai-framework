## What we are deciding

Whether to adopt a written AI use policy for [PROGRAM NAME]. You do not need to
know how AI works to decide this. It is a decision about exposure we already
have, obligations we already carry, and revenue we are currently leaving behind.

## The choice is not whether we use AI

Staff very likely already are. The realistic choice is between governed use and
ungoverned use. A blanket prohibition does not stop the use; it moves it onto
personal phones and free consumer accounts, with children's names in it, outside
any tool register, any written agreement, and any disclosure log. That leaves us
the same exposure, minus the ability to see it, plus a written policy proving we
knew better. **A blanket ban is not a safety posture. It is an unmonitored one.**

## The obligations are already ours

Our family contracts, our own privacy policy and our licensing requirements
already govern what happens to information about children. AI use engages those
obligations; it does not invent new ones. [IF WE ARE A HEAD START GRANTEE: 45
CFR §1303.22 governs third-party disclosure, §1303.23(e) gives any parent the
right to review our written agreements with third parties, and §1303.24 requires
a disclosure log.] The policy is how we keep meeting what we already owe.

## There is money on the table

Subsidy billing that never quite reconciles, infant slots sitting empty while
the waitlist goes uncalled, and grants we never apply for. A program that
forbids AI outright leaves that on the table while staff take the risks anyway.

## What we are asking you to approve

1. The attached AI use policy, as written.
2. Three risk tiers, with authority to approve Tier 1 uses (no child or family
   data) delegated to [ROLE]; Tier 2 to [ROLE]; Tier 3 to [ROLE OR BOARD] in
   writing.
3. An Approved Tools Register maintained by [ROLE] and reported to you
   [QUARTERLY].
4. Annual review of this policy, first review [DATE].

## What this does not commit us to

- **No spending.** Approval authorizes a policy, not a purchase. Any tool with a
  cost returns to you under our normal threshold of [$AMOUNT].
- **No cameras, no classroom surveillance, no biometric check-in.**
- **No tool that touches an individual child** without separate written family
  consent.
- **No endorsement of any vendor**, and nothing permanent: any tool can be
  stopped under our Incident and Discontinuation Protocol.

Approved by [NAME], [ROLE], on [DATE]. Recorded at [MINUTE REFERENCE].
