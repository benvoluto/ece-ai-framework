**Does the state's student-privacy law reach pre-K or child care?** Unresolved.
Illinois has a student online personal protection act, SOPPA, and whether it
reaches preschool at all could not be confirmed for this draft. That is one of
the two open questions below, and it is the reason this row cannot be treated as
settled either way.

**Is there a biometric statute with a private right of action?** Illinois has a
biometric statute — BIPA, the Biometric Information Privacy Act. It is the
statute that any question about biometrics in Illinois runs through. How BIPA
applies to an early childhood program specifically, and the mechanics of who may
enforce it, have not been verified for this page.
