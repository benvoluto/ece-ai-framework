Some Head Start programs are themselves educational agencies covered by FERPA.
In practice these are usually the district-operated ones — but who appears at the
top of the org chart is not the test.

45 CFR §1303.21(a) directs a program that receives funds under a Department of
Education program, and is therefore subject to FERPA, to comply with FERPA
instead of the Head Start records subpart. The trigger is a funding stream.

So the question to ask about your own program is not "are we part of a school
district?" It is "do we receive funds under a Department of Education program?"
A grantee can answer no to the first and yes to the second, and the answer to the
second is the one that moves the program from the row below into this one.
