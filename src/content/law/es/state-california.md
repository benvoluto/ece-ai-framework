**Does the state's student-privacy law reach pre-K or child care?** Yes, through
a statute most templates miss. AB 2799 (2016) added the Early Learning Personal
Information Protection Act at Business and Professions Code §22586, covering
operators of services used and marketed primarily for preschool and pre-K
purposes, with biometric information expressly inside "covered information." A
California early-learning vendor may be subject to ELPIPA and not to SOPIPA. For
district-run pre-K, Education Code §49073.1 also governs the district's
contracts with education technology vendors. Both statutes regulate the vendor,
so the contract is where a program uses them.

**Is there a biometric statute with a private right of action?** Not established
here. ELPIPA expressly counts biometric information as covered information, but
that is a constraint on early-learning operators, not an answer to this
question. What other California law does about biometrics, and whether anything
in it can be enforced by a family directly, has not been verified for this page.
